## CROSS-PLATFORM STRATEGY

### Achievement Collision Rules
When same achievement exists on multiple platforms (e.g., Elden Ring on Steam, Xbox, PS):

1. **First claim wins** - Earliest unlock date gets vintage bonus
2. **Only one forge per achievement** - Can't forge Steam AND Xbox Malenia
3. **Platform-specific items stay exclusive** - Bloodborne only from PSN

### Provider Priority for Item Design
1. **Steam** - Most data, most players, primary focus
2. **PlayStation** - Exclusive titles (Bloodborne, Demon's Souls)
3. **Battle.net** - WoW has unique achievement culture
4. **Xbox** - Halo exclusives, Game Pass achievements
5. **Social** - Prestige badges only (legacy/unobtainable)
6. **Roblox** - Tenure-focused, limited game-specific
