Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 21, 2025

1. Introduction

Ashbane ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our game and authentication services at ashbane.net.

2. Information We Collect

When you sign in through a third-party provider, we collect:

  • Basic Profile Information: Your username/display name, email address, and profile picture from the authentication provider you choose (Steam, Discord, Xbox, PlayStation, GitHub, Facebook, etc.)
  • Gaming Achievements: Your public achievement data from connected gaming platforms, used to unlock in-game items
  • Account Identifiers: Unique identifiers from each provider to link your accounts

We do not collect:

  • Payment information or financial data
  • Private messages or social media posts
  • Friends lists or contact information
  • Location data beyond what's in your public profile

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Create and manage your Ashbane account
  • Authenticate you across our game and web services
  • Calculate your achievement tier and unlock corresponding items
  • Display your profile information in-game and on leaderboards
  • Prevent fraud and abuse of our services

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data only in these circumstances:

  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly agree to share information
  • Service Providers: With trusted services that help us operate (hosting, analytics), bound by confidentiality agreements
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights and safety

5. Third-Party Authentication

When you log in via Steam, Discord, Xbox, PlayStation, GitHub, Facebook, or other providers, their respective privacy policies also apply. We encourage you to review their policies:

  • Steam Privacy Policy
  • Discord Privacy Policy
  • Xbox/Microsoft Privacy Policy
  • PlayStation Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Facebook Privacy Policy

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure session management, and access controls. However, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us. Achievement credit records may be retained to prevent abuse of our systems.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Disconnect any linked provider accounts
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

9. Children's Privacy

Ashbane is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting a notice on our website or through the game client.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@ashbane.net
  • Discord: discord.gg/ashbane
