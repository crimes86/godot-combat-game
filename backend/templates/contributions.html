🧵 What is the Tapestry?

The Tapestry is where achievements from different gaming realms are woven together.

How it works:

  • Weave Threads — Match equivalent achievements across platforms (e.g., Steam ↔ Xbox)
  • Approve Patterns — Vote on others' mappings to confirm they're correct
  • Earn Titles — Threadbinder (5), Loomkeeper (25), Tapestry Master (100)

Why does this matter?

Cross-platform mappings let Ashbane recognize the same achievement whether you unlocked it on Steam, Xbox, or PlayStation.

You can only weave threads for games where you have achievements — your experience qualifies you.

Each provider forms its own thread. Here, we weave them together—
mapping achievements across realms to form the living Tapestry of Ashbane.
