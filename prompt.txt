### 💎 **The Unique Combat System:**

**Crit Windows (Global):**
- When enemies take 100% damage threshold, they enter "crit window"
- Enemy grows to 2.8x scale, glows, becomes vulnerable
- ALL players see this simultaneously (global event)

**Weakpoints (Personal):**
- Each player sees 3 unique weakpoint positions on the enemy
- Players must destroy THEIR OWN weakpoints (individual skill check)
- Positions are different for each player (no fighting over same targets)

**Chain System (Shared):**
- Every weakpoint destroyed by ANY player adds +1 to SHARED team chain
- Chain multiplier benefits all players
- Higher chains = massive damage bonuses
- Individual mistakes don't break chain (forgiving design)

**Why this system is brilliant:**
- Everyone participates (global windows)
- Individual skill still matters (personal weakpoints)
- Team coordination is rewarded (shared chain)
- Scales from 2 to 40+ players beautifully
